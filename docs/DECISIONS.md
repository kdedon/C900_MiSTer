# Scaffold decisions

Every scaffolding choice, with its source. OPEN marks a question the
sources do not settle; answer it, do not guess. Planning documents named
here (`*_PLAN.md` and the like, k8000 docs) are the private design notes
behind a row and are not part of this repository; the row itself is the
record.

## Clocks

| ID | Decision | Why / source |
|---|---|---|
| D-CLK-1 | One master clock, `clk_sys` = 48 MHz from the user PLL (`rtl/pll`, 50 MHz in, output 0 only). Every machine flop runs on it except the video dot sides, in `clk_vid`: the LR card's (D-VID-1) and the HR serialiser `hr_video` (D-VID-3). Global clocks: this one, the framework's (SDRAM, DDRAM, HDMI, audio, HPS) and `clk_vid` (`pll_vid`, reprogrammed per card, D-CLK-7). No gated, divided or per-chip clock, no clock-control block, no second machine PLL. | User rule 2026-11-02 (as the Go simulator: osc12 -> ls93 + fantdiv make every chip clock pin). 48 MHz = 4 x 12 MHz: Y1 changes every second master clock; every LS93 rate is exact. |
| D-CLK-2 | (2026-11-02, pulse convention.) Y1 (`rtl/clock/osc12`): mod-4 phase counter on the master clock, one-master-clock pulses at its rise (phase 2) and fall (phase 0). U14 74LS93 (`rtl/clock/ls93`, Go ls93; QA tied to CKB, R0 Low): each stage toggles in the master clock after its input's fall pulse; its rise/fall pulses come from flops set in the same clock, high while the stage shows the new level. QA 6 MHz = ZCLK (CPU and MMU CLK through U15 H3006A; CN9/CN11 _CLK); CLK = QA inverted by U13 (its rise is QA's fall: PCLK of SCC U74 and U53; CIO U70 counts on the CLK fall = QA's rise); QD 750 kHz SNDCLK (CIO U66 PCLK fall). One master clock of ripple per stage, as before; each pulse is high in exactly the clock the old `x & ~x_q` detector was true, so every consumer's timing is unchanged (FPGA_SIMPLIFICATION_REVIEW F1). Power-up state zero, no reset: the tree runs through the machine reset. | Go sim chips/osc12, chips/ls93; C900 CLOCK_PLAN.md FPGA section |
| D-CLK-3 | (2026-11-02, third revision.) Every chip clock pin takes one-master-clock pulses from the board divider (CLOCK_PLAN.md "FPGA: one master clock, clock pins as edge pulses"; k8000 HANDOFF_2026-11-02_REAL_PINS): `<pin>` for one edge, `<pin>_rise`/`<pin>_fall` for both. | D-CLK-4 | The hard disk card's 8049 runs on the stock ZCLK edges (CN9 _CLK), not the CPU clock. | CLOCK_PLAN.md CLK-Q1: 8049 XTAL = host _CLK. Separate from the CPU clock so a turbo CPU leaves it at 6 MHz. |
| D-CLK-5 | Off-board crystals: phase accumulators on the master clock (`rtl/clock/fantdiv`), each edge a pulse. Y101 10 MHz (5/12 edges per master clock; WD10C20 XTAL, the ST-506 emulator's 50 ns step); KBD 2 MHz (one rise every 24 master clocks, at master clock 13 mod 24 from power-up; 6500/1 XTLI, Phase 2 = 1 MHz, D-KBD-1; the Go simulator's fantdiv still runs 1 MHz); RTC 32.768 kHz (64/46875 edges per master clock, rise pulse to the MSM58321 stub). Not C900 parts: separate oscillators with no phase relation to Y1, so an accumulator on the master clock is as faithful as one on Y1 edges. Y101 edge spacing differs from the simulator's (2 or 3 master clocks vs 4/2/2/2/2, same mean 2.4); no gate step compares it. | C900 FPGA_SIMPLIFICATION_REVIEW F1; Go sim chips/fantdiv |
| D-CLK-6 | NON-STOCK turbo (`c900_turbo`): the OSD CPU clock only sets where the CPU's CLK rise and fall pulses fire (D-CLK-9): the ZCLK edges (6 MHz, stock), a phase accumulator (10 MHz), every ZCLK edge (12 MHz), every master clock (48 MHz; the k8000 CPU is gated only by its pulses, k8000 tb_cpu_mmu_clk passes at 1:1). ZCLK, QA-QD and every other chip stay on the stock tree. The stock board inserts no wait states (/WAIT driven only by LR U13 and the HR PALs; DRAM, ROM and peripherals meet 6 MHz timing). Above 6 MHz the turbo block drives the same /WAIT line (wire-AND, like an expansion card) while the CPU owns the bus: DRAM cycles until the controller's ack (no write pending when the next cycle starts), other memory 4 master clocks from the /DS fall; never while /MREQ is High (2026-11-05: the aborted fetch that opens an interrupt has no /DS, the block held /WAIT forever and the kernel stopped at its first interrupt at 12 and 48 MHz). While ST shows I/O, special I/O or an acknowledge, the CLK pulse falls back to the ZCLK rises, so those cycles run at stock timing (2026-11-04: the SCC and CIOs serve /AS and /DS at PCLK rises; at 48 MHz a memory cycle's /DS rise and the next I/O /DS fall shared one PCLK period, the /DS rise cancelled the read drive and every SCC/CIO read returned FF). That T1 opens on a fast rise and ends at the first ZCLK rise at least 8 master clocks later (2026-11-04 night: a T1 that began one master clock before a ZCLK rise left /AS one clock wide, the CIO missed its select, PACS read FF, and the BIOS key poll after the memory test never ended at 48 MHz). CLK also stays on the ZCLK rises while the bus is granted and up to the first ZCLK rise after it is back (2026-11-04 night: the 8716 drops /BUSREQ at its last cycle's T2; at 48 MHz the CPU raised /BUSACK two master clocks later and retook the bus inside that cycle, the 8049's read of the HD status word was cut and the HD boot failed with status 0xFF). Above 6 MHz U53 (CAS/MUX) is clocked every master clock while the CPU drives the bus (board: by CLK; a faster CPU starts its next /DS before CLK would raise CAS, and the DRAM would miss the next column strobe); during DMA it keeps the stock CLK the 8716 is timed to. DMA guard (USER DECISION 2026-11-05): the SCC, the CIOs and the MMU keep the select latched at the last /AS rise and the 8716 has no /AS, so a DMA /DS right after an I/O, special I/O or acknowledge cycle reaches the chip that cycle selected (the stock board has the same exposure; at 6 MHz the BIOS never meets it; at 48 MHz an HD status word went into SCC WR0 as 0x4F and the console stopped). Above 6 MHz, from an /AS fall with an I/O-class status to the next /AS rise with any other status (U57 is off then, so every peripheral latches a deselected CS0; U58 enables it only for ST 0010), the board does not see /BUSACK and the CPU does not see /BUSREQ. The CPU samples /BUSREQ at T1, so withholding /BUSREQ alone cannot stop a grant after an I/O cycle whose T1 saw it Low; with /BUSACK masked too, the CPU then sees /BUSREQ High, takes the bus back two CLK rises later and runs its next cycle first. A board-side dummy /AS was the alternative: it needs the accelerator to drive /AS and ST on the board. Speed 0: both lines pass straight through. USER DECISION 2026-11-02: turbo /WAIT block accepted as a turbo-only non-stock expansion; inactive at 6 MHz (stock timing unchanged). Not verified on hardware; Verilator runs in BUILD_STATUS. | FPGA_SIMPLIFICATION_REVIEW F9; k8000 z8000_bus_unit (/WAIT sampled at CLK rises in T3) |
| D-CLK-7 | Video: `CLK_VIDEO` = `clk_vid`, sole output of `pll_vid`, reprogrammed for the fitted card (D-VID-3): LR 65.028 MHz (D-VID-1), HR 96 MHz. `CE_PIXEL` from `video_mixer`: LR one clk_vid in four, HR mono every clock, HR colour one in four. | MiSTer contract `CLK_VIDEO > 40 MHz`, 4x ce_pix for the scandoubler. |
| D-CLK-8 | MMU edge order (data sheet). Z8010 data sheet (docs/zilog/z8010_mmu.pdf, pp.571-573): /AS pin p.571 "The rising edge of AS indicates that AD0-AD15, ST0-ST3, R/W and N/S are valid"; #13/#14 offset setup/hold to the /AS rise; #18/#19 SN to the CLK rise; #36 /AS rise at least 10 ns before the CLK rise; #29 CLK rise to A8-A23 valid. On the board CLK is one net with the CPU's (U1.35, U2.36), no delayed copy. The k8000 MMU (working tree, O-26) latches offset and status at its /AS rise seen on the master clock and drives PA combinationally, so PA is valid before the CPU's next CLK rise at every ratio; the core gives the MMU the CPU's CLK pulse and CPU-side strobes. Closes O-26. | z8010_mmu.pdf pp.571-573; k8000 HANDOFF O-26 |
| D-CLK-9 | (2026-11-07) CPU CLK as a rise and fall pulse pair (k8000 09abc0b): the Z8001 takes `clk_rise` and `clk_fall`, the Z8010 the rise (its /AS rise latches). On the fall: /AS rises, /MREQ and the write and I/O /DS fall, /DS rises at T3's fall, /WAIT and STOP are sampled. Edges per OSD speed (`O[7:6]`, conf v,9): 0 = 6 MHz, the ZCLK edges; 1 = 10 MHz, a phase accumulator (+5 mod 12 per master clock, an edge on each wrap), halves of 3,2,3,2,2 master clocks (5 periods in 24); 2 = 12 MHz, rise at every ZCLK edge, fall 2 master clocks later; 3 = 48 MHz, a rise in every master clock and a fall in every one with CLK High, the CPU taking the pair as fall then rise. Every half lasts 2 or more master clocks except at 48 MHz. In the stock fallback (I/O-class cycles, grants) T1 rises at a ZCLK rise 8 or more master clocks after the last rise and falls at a ZCLK fall 4 or more after its rise: with /AS 2 master clocks Low U56 had not latched the address and the MMU took /CS from the previous one (a dropped descriptor byte, CPU-Inhibit, sizing trap). Single-step (D-RTC-1) counts stock CLK cycles; at 48 MHz it steps twice: unsupported. One master clock, no second CPU clock (a fractional PLL was rejected). | 1988 Data Book p.40; tb/core gate step 8 |
| D-CLK-10 | (2026-11-07, user; LC card C1) LC card (8563) clock: `clk_vdc` = 32 MHz, third output of the main PLL `pll` (VCO 960 MHz: C 20 = clk_sys 48, C 10 = clk_ram 96, C 30 = clk_vdc 32) no divider logic; the vendored VDC makes its 16 MHz dot by its own divide by 2. One VCO, so clk_sys and clk_vdc rises coincide every 62.5 ns and the Z-Bus to 8563 boundary is a related-clock crossing timed by STA (`derive_pll_clocks`; both in the `pll` clock group of C900.sdc, no exception, no multicycle; worst edge pair 10.4 ns): plain registers, no synchroniser. An access is a four-phase level handshake: `req_q` (clk_sys) rises at the second /DS-low clock with RS, R/W and data held until the /DS rise; clk_vdc gives the 8563 one /CS + bus strobe clock and raises `ack_vq` on the clock `db_out` takes the read byte; reset crosses as one register. /WAIT is kept (an access needs 5-6 master clocks, 104-125 ns, from the /DS fall, measured in tb/lc_display at all three clk phases and with one-clock turbo strobes): a 6 MHz CPU samples /WAIT about 12 master clocks after the /DS fall, so at stock speed it never holds the cycle; it stretches turbo I/O only. Replaces the toggle handshake through c900_sync2 (up to 13 clocks, 271 ns). | user 2026-11-07; LC_CARD_8563.md sec 3 |
| D-CLK-11 | (2026-11-07) `pll_vid` LC setting: M 19.2, C0 15 = 64 MHz (4 x the 16 MHz dot), `vid_sel` 0 LR, 1 HR, 2 LC; two 2FF bits and a two-sample agreement filter in CLK_50M; `vid_ready` compares with `{cfg_lc, cfg_hr}`. Extends D-CLK-7. | LC_CARD_8563.md V2 |
| D-CLK-12 | (2026-11-12) NON-STOCK turbo, card reads at 48 MHz: `c900_turbo` holds /WAIT one master clock past the cards' /WAIT release on a read cycle at 48 MHz outside the stock fallback (`card_wait_q`), and pauses CLK between a card cycle and the next (below); 6, 10 and 12 MHz, writes' /WAIT and I/O-class cycles unchanged. Root cause: every chip sees the bus through the fabric capture register `ad_q`, one master clock behind the drivers, and the Z8001 reads AD from it, but /WAIT reaches the CPU live. The CPU takes its read word at the CLK rise after the fall that sampled /WAIT High. At 6 MHz that rise comes 4 master clocks later, at 10 and 12 MHz 2 or more, so the word is already in `ad_q`. At 48 MHz fall and rise share one master clock, and a card that releases /WAIT in the clock it drives its word (the HR PALs: SRV) had the CPU take the previous bus value (FFFF): the boot ROM's HR probe failed and fell back to LR, then serial. The DRAM ack already carries this clock (`ack_q`); the LR CDR delays its own release for it (inferred U13). Read path only: a write's card takes AD from `ad_q` before it releases /WAIT, and the CPU holds the word through the /DS rise, which the card sees a clock later. The live /WAIT still holds the CPU in the clock a card first pulls it Low; the registered copy only extends the release. Second, at 48 MHz /MREQ is High for one master clock between cycles, and the HR ZARB clears SRV only when its 24M edge (every second master clock) sees the select High: back-to-back bitmap reads (LDL, LDM) were served from the previous cycle's row. After a cycle in which a card held /WAIT, `c900_turbo` gives the CPU no CLK edge in the first clock /MREQ is High (`gap48`), so the select is High for two master clocks. Cards' logic unchanged. | tb/core gate step 13 (cardrw.s, CPUMON, hr_rmw.s at 48 MHz); before: HR 1532 of 1536 CPU reads wrong at 48 MHz, hr_rmw.s 7271; with the /WAIT clock alone hr_rmw.s still read 85 words from the wrong row |
| D-CLK-13 | (2026-11-12, user approved) NON-STOCK turbo, SCC and CIO recovery time: above 6 MHz `c900_turbo` holds /WAIT on every CPU cycle until /DS has been High for the valid access recovery time since the last access to an SCC (U74, the LR card's U31/U36) or a CIO (U70, U66): SCC 6 TcPC + 200 ns = 58 master clocks at the 6 MHz PCLK (Z8030 data sheet, z8030_scc.pdf p.17 (printed 647), parameter 14 TrC, 4 MHz column; p.15 (645): from the /DS rise of one SCC transaction to the /DS fall of the next), CIO 1000 ns = 48 master clocks (Z8036 data sheet, z8036_cio.pdf p.20 (692), parameter 21 Trc, 4 MHz column, note 3: /DS rise of one CIO access to /DS fall of another). The slower part's column, since the fitted grade is not known. /WAIT cannot delay an I/O cycle's own /DS fall (T2), so the hold lands on the cycles between: every Z8000 instruction fetches before its I/O cycle, and the block I/O instructions alternate with a memory cycle. One window for all five chips (the next cycle's target is not known when it is held). The CIO figure is in ns and is applied to U66 too, although its PCLK is 750 kHz. 6 MHz unchanged: the stock board meets the times by construction (gate step 14 measures them at 6 MHz as well). | tb/core gate step 14 (iorec.s, IOREC) |
| D-CLK-14 | (2026-11-15, user decision) NON-STOCK turbo, 48 MHz /WAIT and STOP: the CPU's /WAIT and STOP pins come from master-clock flops with no logic after them. `c900_turbo` drives /WAIT from `wait48_q` (a static speed select picks it). STOP, at every speed, is the FPU's /BSY, a flop since k8000 934389c, ORed only with the static FPU enable (`cfg_fpu`, loaded while _MRESET is Low). The /WAIT flop takes next clock's line: the turbo terms from their next state, /MREQ falling after a T1 rise, and each card's /WAIT one clock ahead (`wait_nx`: LR `lr_cdr` served, HR ZARB SRV next, LC done next; LANCE and APU give their live /WAIT). 6, 10 and 12 MHz keep the combinational lines (the CPU registers them itself). Turbo-board logic under the schematic-as-built rule. ## Reset

| ID | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| D-RST-1 | `RESET` (framework, asynchronous) through a 2FF synchroniser; one register ORs it with `ioctl_download` (2026-11-05: `status[0]` and `buttons[1]` are S1, D-RST-4); that register asynchronously asserts `c900_rst_sync`, whose 2FF release is gated by `pll_locked`. One polarity (`rst_n`) thereafter. | PDMAC_PATTERN_REVIEW.md precondition 1; MiSTer manual sec 2; HDL guideline sec 2 (async source must be a register). |
| D-RST-2 | Core reset = board power-on: U59 NE556 section 2 pulls _MRESET and _PRESET Low (SP sheet 6; the 0.56 s stretch only delays boot, not modelled). _MRESET resets the CPU, MMU, 8716, CN9 and 8723. The 6508's RESET net has no driver on the sheet (ISSUES 35: probably _MRESET or _PRESET); the core uses _MRESET (O-4). _PRESET gates the buffered strobes: _BAS = _AS AND _PRESET, _BDS = _DS AND _PRESET (U11, sheet 1; schematics repo ARCHEOLOGY 2l), so at power-on every buffered-bus chip sees /AS and /DS Low together: the reset of SCC U74, CIO U70, CIO U66 and LR U31, which lack a reset pin (Z8030 TwRES 250 ns over two PCLK rises; Z8036 TwRES 170 ns). The clock tree runs through reset, so the SCCs see PCLK rises. Z8001 and Z8010 take the raw strobes (before U11). MMU /CS = AD1 AND _MRESET (D-RST-5). The 1984 Design Review's "-BAS/-BDS held high during -PRESET" contradicts the traced U11 ANDs; the trace wins. | schematic trace ARCHEOLOGY 2l, DERIVED (U11.1/.13/.9 on ~PRESET); k8000 HANDOFF (SCC/CIO reset by /AS+/DS) |
| D-RST-3 | Firmware download holds the machine in reset. | MiSTer convention (both edges of `ioctl_download`). |
| D-RST-4 | (2026-11-05, user: "S1 + re-read OSD") OSD `R[0]` and the MiSTer button (`buttons[1]`) are switch S1 (sheet 6). S1 reaches TRG1 of U59 section 1 through C86 (R22), so a press fires it once however long it is held; OUT1 stays High 1.1 x R23 510K x C87 1uF = 0.561 s (26,928,000 master clocks) and pulls _MRESET Low through U60, wire-ORed with OUT2. _PRESET stays High: SCCs and CIOs keep their state; the MMU /CS is held Low (D-RST-5). _MRESET consumers (schematic net list; RTL): Z8001 U1.16, Z8010 U2.5, U11.9 MMU /CS (INFERRED, D-RST-5), U11.4 STEP clear and U64.13 REN (`c900_sstep`), keyboard shifter U71.9 (`kbdshifter`), 8716 U79.32, CN9.30 to the HDC (`DU2` / WD2010 MR), 8723 U204.36 (with the 6508, O-4), CN11.5 (LANCE, FPU stubs), LR U9 HD6545 /RESET and U23 CLR (`ctl_q`), HR U312 CLR, `rtc_seed`. _PRESET: U11.1, U11.13 (_BAS, _BDS), CN11.3 (no card uses it). Host side, not on the board: the OSD options (display card, HR mode, RAM size, CPU clock, LANCE, FPU) are sampled while _MRESET is Low, so they change at an S1 press as at power-on; the core reset (`rst_n`) is untouched. A new display card retunes pll_vid: during the pulse a mismatch does not reset the core, the card stays out of the slot (`card_fitted`) until pll_vid locks (a few ms); still unlocked when the pulse ends, the core resets as before. Disks stay mounted (drives see no reset). Cold load (2026-11-06): Main holds status[0] (S1) from the core load through its init, which fired U59 and left the MMU with MSEN = 0 (CPU read FFFF); `hps_rst` (C900.sv) holds reset until status[0] has gone 1 then 0, `s1_q` resets to 1; MiSTer cold load 5/5. | MB sheet 6 (MB_S06_Reset); tb/core gate.sh step 10 |
| D-RST-5 | (2026-11-06, user) INFERRED, pending a continuity check: U11.9, the MMU /CS gate input, is _MRESET, not _PRESET. MMU /CS = latched AD1 AND _MRESET, so S1 as well as power-on holds /CS Low and the Z8010 leaves reset in pass-through (MSEN = 1). With _PRESET the Z8001 3-states AD during an S1 reset, BAD1 floats High, MSEN = 0 and the CPU reads FFFF: no S1 press after a kernel ran rebooted. With _MRESET, 10 of 10 S1 presses rebooted on the MiSTer. | user 2026-11-06; MiSTer S1 test 2026-11-06 |
